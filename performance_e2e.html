Method notes

  • Environment: full MLE-bench-legal stack (torch, torchvision, transformers, timm, one GPU per program, pretrained models allowed). Verified from traces: 0% of old-corpus programs used deep learning; 44–50% of new-corpus programs do.
  • All arms share one verbatim improvement mandate; only the feedback block differs.
  • The WM judge prompt is byte-identical to codeworld-gym's deployed reward prompt (test-enforced), and rubrics are rendered exactly as the deployment database ships them.
